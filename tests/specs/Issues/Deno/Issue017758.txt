!! should keep blank lines between paragraphs of a list item in a block quote !!
> 1. First paragraph.
>
>    Second paragraph.

[expect]
> 1. First paragraph.
>
>    Second paragraph.

!! should keep blank lines with multiple paragraphs and an unordered list !!
> - First paragraph.
>
>   Second paragraph.
>
>   Third paragraph.

[expect]
> - First paragraph.
>
>   Second paragraph.
>
>   Third paragraph.

!! should keep blank lines in a nested block quote list !!
> > 1. First paragraph.
> >
> >    Second paragraph.

[expect]
>> 1. First paragraph.
>>
>>    Second paragraph.

!! should still merge soft wrapped lines without a blank line in a block quote list !!
> 1. First paragraph
>    still first paragraph.

[expect]
> 1. First paragraph
>    still first paragraph.

!! should keep the blank line between items of a loose list in a block quote !!
> - a
>
> - b
>
> - c

[expect]
> - a
>
> - b
>
> - c

!! should keep the blank line before a sublist in a block quote !!
> - a
>
>   - sub

[expect]
> - a
>
>   - sub

!! should keep the blank line before a paragraph following a sublist in a block quote !!
> - first
>   - sub one
>   - sub two
>
>   Trailing paragraph of the parent item.

[expect]
> - first
>   - sub one
>   - sub two
>
>   Trailing paragraph of the parent item.

!! should keep blank lines between items of a nested ordered list in a block quote !!
> 1. a
>
>    1. sub1
>
>    2. sub2

[expect]
> 1. a
>
>    1. sub1
>
>    2. sub2

!! should not treat a literal greater than sign as a blank line in a block quote !!

> - c
